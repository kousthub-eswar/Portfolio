Point-of-Sale & Retail Management System

A mobile-first PWA for small retail vendors with camera barcode scanning, inventory tracking, and customer credit ledger management.

  • Modular service architecture covered by automated Vitest suites; auto-deployed on Vercel

TuneSense

Explainable Music Recommendation System

A full-stack recommendation engine combining six composable algorithms with transparent, human-readable explanations for every suggested track.

  • 6 composable recommendation strategies: popularity, content, behavioral, contextual, mood, collaborative
  • 11 Mongoose schemas with materialized views, append-only event logs & telemetry aggregation
  • Cosine-similarity peer matching on acoustic vectors with offline evaluation (NDCG, Precision@K)
  • Hardened security with JWT sessions in HttpOnly cookies, bcrypt hashing, and Zod validation
🎧 6 Composable Engines 🧠 11 Mongoose Schemas

Audit Analytics System

Enterprise Ledger Forensic Anomaly Detection

A quantitative forensic audit pipeline analyzing enterprise financial ledgers to detect accounting irregularities, fraud patterns, and control evasions.
